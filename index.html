✨ Features

Everything Your Group Actually Needs

Not another messaging app. A living, breathing social space that understands your group.

🤖

ROBO — Your AI Host

A personality-driven AI mascot that greets you by name, remembers your history, narrates sessions like a sports commentator, and interrupts boring moments with games.

👻

Ghost Crown System

Silent too long? AI detects it, crowns you with a hilarious title like "Lord of the AFK Realm," and drops a dare. Complete it to redeem yourself — or the crown gets bigger.

🎲

Chaos Cards

Once per session, any member can play a Chaos Card to completely flip the format. Become the co-creator of your group experience, not just a participant.

🚨

Boredom Engine

When ECHO detects dead chat, ROBO deploys Emergency Fun Protocol — auto-launching mini-games like Lightning Round, Hot Seat Roulette, and Plot Twist.

⚖️

AI Judge

After dares and games, ROBO transforms into Judge Mode. The jury votes, ROBO delivers a hilarious verdict, and losers join the Wall of Shame in the Memory Palace.

🔮

Prophecy System

At session start, ROBO makes wild predictions about your group. At the end, check which ones came true. Hilarious accountability and inside joke fuel.
